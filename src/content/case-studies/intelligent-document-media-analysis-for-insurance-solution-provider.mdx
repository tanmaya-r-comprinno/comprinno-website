## Overview

## **Overview**

**About Client**

A technology driven insurance solutions provider that specializes in delivering digital platforms for policy administration and claims processing. The company focuses on delivering seamless and efficient insurance workflows for partners and end users. To overcome manual, time intensive claims processes, the organization implemented an AI-powered solution to reduce human intervention, shorten claim turnaround times, and enhance accuracy across the claims lifecycle.

## The Challenge

## **Challenges**

The insurance solutions provider was looking to elevate its claims operations by introducing greater speed, accuracy, and consistency across the end-to-end workflow. With a growing volume of unstructured inputs such as driving licenses, vehicle documents, policy forms, photos, and videos the team saw an opportunity to move beyond manual review and adopt intelligent automation. They wanted a streamlined way to extract structured information from documents, ensure reliable interpretation of claim-related images and videos, and support more standardized cause-of-loss assessment. The existing processes operated through multiple independent steps, creating scope for a more unified and scalable framework that could handle increasing demand. Additionally, the organization aimed to enhance the internal team experience by enabling automated, context-aware query handling. Together, these aspirations highlighted the need for an AI-driven platform capable of harmonizing document processing, video analysis, and conversational support in a cohesive, future-ready solution.

## The Solution

## **Solution**

To address the need for a more intelligent, accurate, and scalable claims ecosystem, a comprehensive AI-driven platform was designed using Amazon Bedrock and complementary AWS services. The solution combines document understanding, image and media analysis, and conversational intelligence into a unified workflow that greatly reduces manual effort. Bedrock Data Automation (BDA) with custom blueprints was implemented to extract structured information from licenses, vehicle documents, and claim forms, ensuring consistent and high-precision data capture. For image and video based assessments, state-of-the-art foundation models were used to interpret vehicle damage, analyze claim photos and videos, and support a standardized cause-of-loss evaluation process. A Bedrock Agent integrated with a Knowledge Base enables an intelligent chatbot capable of handling claim-related queries and providing contextual guidance to users. All components were orchestrated within a cohesive architecture that enhances operational efficiency, improves decision quality, and establishes a future-ready foundation for advanced insurance automation

### **Key Actions:**

**Automated Document Extraction with BDA :**

- Designed and configured custom BDA blueprints tailored for licenses, vehicle documents.

- Enabled accurate extraction of structured fields from unstructured images and PDFs.

- Developed a flexible extraction pipeline that can also read chassis numbers, license plates, and additional document formats as needed.

 

**Image-Based Damage Analysis :**

- Integrated Claude 3.7 Sonnet for initial damage extraction from claim images and Claude 4 Sonnet and Nova Premier for comparative analysis and validation.

- Implemented multi-model evaluation using Claude and Nova variants for improved reliability and cross-verification.

- Standardized outputs into structured formats to support consistent claim assessment workflows.

 

**Intelligent Chatbot with Bedrock Agents :**

- Configured a Bedrock Agent to orchestrate claim-related tasks and automate guided workflows.

- Connected a Knowledge Base for contextual retrieval from policy documents and claim information.

- Enabled natural-language interactions for answering user queries and assisting with claim processes

 

**Unified End-to-End Architecture :**

- Integrated document extraction, image analysis, and chatbot capabilities into a seamless workflow.

- Created standardized API interfaces enabling smooth data exchange across modules.

- Ensured the architecture remains extensible for future AI insurance use cases.

 

**API Integration & Orchestration :**

- Used AWS Lambda to orchestrate requests, call Bedrock models, and manage inter-service communication.

- Configured API Gateway with authentication, request/response validation, and throttling to ensure secure and efficient operation.

**Scalability, Accuracy & Efficiency :**

- Reduced manual effort by automating high-volume, repetitive claim processing activities.

- Improved accuracy and consistency through standardized AI-driven evaluations.

- Delivered a scalable foundation capable of supporting increased claim volumes and evolving business needs.

Comprinno designed a secure, scalable architecture to support the AI-powered insurance claim automation solution by seamlessly integrating AWS Bedrock with core AWS infrastructure services. Bedrock capabilities including Claude 3.7/4 Sonnet for image-based damage analysis, Bedrock Data Automation for OCR, and Bedrock Agents for conversational insurance workflows are orchestrated through API Gateway and AWS Lambda to deliver AI-driven processing at scale. S3 serves as the secure data lake for storing documents, photos, and videos, while DynamoDB provides low-latency, highly available storage for structured claim data. All components operate within a tightly controlled VPC and are secured using IAM and KMS to ensure regulatory compliance and strong data protection. Monitoring and auditability are achieved using CloudWatch, CloudTrail, and Config. This end-to-end architecture ensures high performance, automation, and accuracy across document extraction, damage assessment, and video analysis, providing insurance solutions provider with a robust and extensible platform for future AI-driven insurance operations.

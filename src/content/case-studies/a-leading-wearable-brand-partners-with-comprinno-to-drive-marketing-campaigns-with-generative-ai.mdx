## Overview

## **Client Overview**

**About Client:**

A leading global wearable brand and India’s top earwear provider offers a range of products through its web portal and mobile application. During the 2020–21 pandemic period, demand for audio wearables grew significantly, resulting in a rapid increase in customers and the development of a strong online community of approximately 3 million users.

## The Challenge

## **Challenges**

A leading brand in the wearable and audio accessories market aimed to launch a highly targeted marketing campaign to enhance customer engagement and retention. The campaign’s success depended on the ability to hyper-personalize emails, ensuring each message was relevant and tailored to individual customers. Specifically, the organization sought to leverage churn analytics data to identify customers at risk of disengagement and deliver personalized offers to re-engage them. To achieve this level of personalization, the campaign required emails to include customer specific details such as the last purchased item, along with customized offers, product-related discounts, and targeted cross-sell and upsell recommendations. This level of complexity went beyond traditional marketing approaches and required an advanced Generative AI (Gen AI) solution capable of processing large volumes of customer data and generating highly personalized content at scale.

The organization required a solution that could automate personalization efficiently, ensuring each customer received relevant and meaningful communication, thereby improving re-engagement rates and reducing churn.

## The Solution

## **Solution**

To address the needs of a leading brand in the wearable and audio accessories market for hyper-personalized marketing campaigns, a sophisticated Generative AI (Gen AI) solution was developed in collaboration with a technology partner, leveraging cloud services to automate and optimize the process 

**Key Actions:**

**Data Storage and Knowledge Base Creation: **

- All relevant campaign data including churn analytics, product brochures, brand guidelines, email templates, logos, images, and marketing offers is securely stored in an Amazon S3 bucket, serving as a centralized repository.

-  This data is integrated into a knowledge base using an AWS Bedrock agent, a fully managed service, enabling efficient retrieval for personalized email generation.

- AWS Bedrock is leveraged for its model comparison capabilities, allowing efficient selection of the most suitable AI model for generating accurate and context-aware content at scale.

 

** Embedding and Vector Database: **

- A managed AI service automates the creation of a knowledge base, enabling the selection of an embedding model to chunk data from cloud storage into smaller, manageable pieces.

- The chunked data is converted into embedding structured representations that allow AI models to efficiently process and retrieve relevant information.

- These embeddings are stored in a vector database, with the platform providing multiple database options; a managed search and analytics service is selected to ensure scalable and efficient retrieval.

 

** Dynamic Email Content Generation: **

- When a marketing team member triggers a prompt through a managed AI agent, it initiates a workflow orchestration service that gathers information from multiple data sources to generate different sections of the marketing email.

- Each section of the email is dynamically created using a Large Language Model (LLM), including personalized content such as the customer’s last purchased item, relevant offers or vouchers, and targeted cross-sell or upsell recommendations.

- The generated content is then stored in a separate database for reuse and further processing, ensuring scalability and efficiency in campaign execution.

 

**Email Campaign Execution: **

- Once the email content is generated and stored, users can either manually send emails or trigger campaigns using a mass email tool, enabling automated dispatch to a large number of recipients.

- This ensures that each customer receives a highly personalized message tailored to their purchasing history and preferences, improving engagement and campaign effectiveness.

- The end-to-end solution automates content creation and campaign execution, allowing a leading brand in the wearable and audio accessories market to efficiently manage large-scale campaigns. The integration of managed AI and cloud services provides a scalable, secure, and robust platform for marketing initiatives, ultimately helping improve customer engagement and reduce churn.

## Results

## **Benefits**

 

- **Enhanced Personalization & Customer Retention** – A leading brand in the wearable and audio accessories market leveraged a GenAI-powered solution to create hyper-personalized email campaigns based on customer purchasing history and preferences. By incorporating churn analytics, the organization effectively targeted at-risk customers with personalized incentives, leading to improved engagement, higher response rates, reduced churn, and stronger customer loyalty.

 

- **Increased Efficiency & Cost Optimization**  – Automation of the email generation and campaign execution process significantly reduced manual effort and turnaround time. This enabled the marketing team to focus more on strategy rather than execution, resulting in faster campaign rollouts while also lowering operational costs and minimizing resource wastage through more precise targeting.

 

- **Revenue Growth through Intelligent Recommendations**  – The ability to incorporate targeted cross-sell and upsell recommendations increased the average order value and improved conversion likelihood by promoting products aligned with customer preferences and purchase behavior.

 

- **Scalability & Data-Driven Decision Making** – The integration of managed AI and cloud services enabled seamless execution of large-scale campaigns, supporting the generation of thousands of personalized emails simultaneously. Additionally, the use of a centralized knowledge base and embedding pipeline improved data organization and retrieval, empowering more informed, insight-driven decision-making.

The implementation of a Generative AI–driven, cloud-enabled solution enabled a leading brand in the wearable and audio accessories market to transform its marketing approach with hyper-personalized, data-driven campaigns at scale.

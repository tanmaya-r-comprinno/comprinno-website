### **Introduction: Why Migrate VMware Workloads to AWS?**

Since Broadcomm’s acquisition of VMware in 2022, many aspects have changed for VMware customers. With Broadcomm CXO’s stating that their focus would shift primarily to their top 20% high-revenue customers who use their enterprise suites. In addition, pricing has gone up considerably. This has left other existing VMware customers – who use other modular offerings –  re-consider their cloud strategy.

While startups and scaleups have consistently chosen AWS because of the stability and services network it offers, Comprinno’s cloud specialists believe migrating VMware workloads to AWS can offer significant advantages – from cost savings, to enhanced security, to resilience and scalability. 

Comprinno’s Migration Services Team leverages Datamotive EasyMigrate to simplify this complex time-consuming process, to ensure a seamless transition to AWS. 

In this blog, we will explore 7 benefits of using EasyMigrate for VMware to AWS migration, making your cloud journey smoother and more efficient.

---

### **1. Reduce Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) by Over 60%**

One of the most compelling reasons to migrate VMware workloads to AWS with EasyMigrate is the potential for substantial cost savings. By eliminating the need for the VMware layer on AWS instances, organizations can achieve a Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) reduction of over 60%. This cost efficiency allows businesses to allocate resources to other critical areas, fostering growth and innovation.

### **2. Online Block-Level Migration with 10-Minute Cutover SLA**

EasyMigrate offers online block-level migration, which ensures minimal downtime during the migration process. With a 10-minute cutover Service Level Agreement (SLA), businesses can transition their workloads with minimal disruption to their operations. This efficiency is crucial for maintaining business continuity and avoiding costly downtime.

### **3. Enhanced Security Posture with Agentless Migration**

Security is a top concern for any organization migrating to the cloud. EasyMigrate enhances your security posture by providing an agentless migration solution. This approach reduces the attack surface and eliminates potential vulnerabilities associated with installing agents on your workloads. As a result, you can migrate with confidence, knowing that your data is secure throughout the process.

### **4. Eliminate VMware Dependency**

Migrating to AWS with EasyMigrate allows you to transform your VMware workloads into native AWS EC2 instances. This transition eliminates the dependency on VMware, enabling you to leverage the full suite of AWS services and features. By adopting a cloud-native approach, you can enhance your infrastructure’s scalability, flexibility, and performance.

### **5. Parallel Migration Capabilities**

[EasyMigrate](https://www.datamotive.io/products/easy-migrate) supports parallel migration, enabling you to migrate multiple virtual machines (VMs) simultaneously. This capability accelerates the migration process, reducing the overall time required to complete the transition. By migrating in parallel, you can ensure a faster deployment and minimize the impact on your business operations.

### **6. Faster Deployment Within 3 Hours**

Time is of essence when it comes to cloud migration. EasyMigrate allows you to deploy and start the migration process within just three hours. This rapid deployment capability ensures that your transition to AWS is swift and efficient, allowing you to reap the benefits of the cloud without unnecessary delays.

### **7. 100% Data Consistency and Resume Replication**

Data integrity is paramount during migration. EasyMigrate guarantees 100% data consistency from the first iteration, reducing the risk of data loss or corruption. Additionally, the solution offers the ability to resume replication from the same point in case of network failure. This feature ensures that your migration process is resilient and reliable, even in the face of unforeseen challenges.

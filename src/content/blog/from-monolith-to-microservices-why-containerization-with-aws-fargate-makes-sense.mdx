### Introduction

Businesses that want to stay competitive must have agility and scalability in the fast-paced digital landscape of today. Microservices are replacing monolithic designs, which can be restrictive and laborious, in many businesses. Using AWS Fargate, a serverless computing engine that lets you run containers without managing servers, is one of the best ways to make this transition. This article explains why using Fargate to containerize monoliths into microservices is a wise choice for businesses trying to improve their operational efficiency, scalability, and flexibility.

#### ss and Increased Security

Fargate only charges for the resources your containers utilize, in contrast to traditional servers. This pay-as-you-go approach lowers the costs that organizations incur from unused resources. Furthermore, Fargate offers integrated security capabilities that enable the safe management of sensitive data, like container isolation and interaction with AWS Identity and Access Management (IAM).

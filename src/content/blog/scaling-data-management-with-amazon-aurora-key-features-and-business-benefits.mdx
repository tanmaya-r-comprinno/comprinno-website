### Key Features of Amazon Aurora

#### High Performance and Scalability

AWS Aurora offers up to five times faster and three times more scalable throughput than normal MySQL and PostgreSQL. Without compromising performance, it automatically expands storage up to 128 TB per database instance as needed.

#### Automatic Backups and Recovery

Aurora provides automated snapshots and continuous backup to Amazon S3, guaranteeing data longevity and simple recovery.

#### Global Database Capabilities

Businesses can set up globally distributed databases with Aurora Global Database, which enables up to five read replicas across regions with low latency and quick recovery.

#### Compatibility with PostgreSQL and MySQL 

Because of its ability to migrate data seamlessly between PostgreSQL and MySQL, Aurora is a flexible option for companies that want to grow without switching database engines.

### **Business Benefits of Amazon Aurora**

Here's a look at the specific business benefits that make Aurora a preferred choice for organizations aiming to optimize their data infrastructure:

- **Cost-Effectiveness:** When compared to traditional databases, Aurora's on-demand pricing model offers enterprises significant cost savings by allowing them to pay only for the usage that they make. 

- **Enhanced Business Agility:** AWS Aurora helps businesses stay flexible and quickly adjust to changing data demands by managing high volumes of transactions and supporting complicated workloads. 

- **High Availability and Reliability:** With a 99.99% uptime SLA, data availability is guaranteed by AWS Aurora's fault-tolerant design and multi-AZ replication.

### FAQs

**Is Amazon Aurora scalable?**Absolutely, Aurora can automatically scale storage up to 128 TB per instance as needed.

**Is Amazon Aurora serverless?**Yes, Amazon Aurora offers a serverless option that automatically adjusts capacity based on demand.

**Does Aurora use EC2?**Yes, Amazon Aurora uses EC2 instances under the hood, but it abstracts this for easier database management.

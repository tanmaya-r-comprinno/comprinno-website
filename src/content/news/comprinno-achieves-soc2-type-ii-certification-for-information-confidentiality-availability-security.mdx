## Announcement
					
## Comprinno achieves SOC2 Type II Certification for Information Confidentiality, Availability & Security
							
							
													
										02nd Dec, 2024
									
						
																	![Image](/images/blog/Comprinno_SOC2.png)													
Comprinno is proud to announce the successful attainment of the SOC 2 Type II certification. As a leading provider of cloud advisory & managed services, the Bengaluru-based company has once again demonstrated its commitment to the highest standards of data security & operational excellence, further strengthening its trust with clients. SOC 2, developed by the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA), is a critical certification that evaluates the controls relevant to security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, & privacy of customer data. Achieving SOC 2 Type II certification highlights Comprinno’s dedication to maintaining rigorous controls over its systems & processes to protect client information.
													![Image](/images/blog/1.png)													
"Commented on this significant achievement: “Achieving SOC 2 Type II certification is a momentous milestone for us. At Comprinno, we understand the paramount importance of data security, & this certification further underscores our commitment to safeguarding our clients’ sensitive information. It serves as a testament to our ongoing efforts to uphold trust & security in everything we do.” The SOC 2 Type II certification process involved an extensive audit by an independent third-party auditor, who reviewed & assessed the company’s policies, procedures, & controls over a defined period. The successful completion of this rigorous process confirms that Comprinno has met the highest standards for securing customer data & maintaining operational integrity."

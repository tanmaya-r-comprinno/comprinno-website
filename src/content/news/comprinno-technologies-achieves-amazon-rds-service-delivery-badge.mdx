## Announcement
					
# Comprinno Technologies achieves Amazon RDS Service Delivery Badge
							
							
													
										07th April, 2025
									
								
													
										Bengaluru, IN
									
						
																	![Image](/images/blog/Amazon_RDS_delivery.png)													
Comprinno Technologies is proud to announce that it has achieved the Amazon Relational Database Service (Amazon RDS) Service Delivery badge, recognizing its expertise in delivering scalable, secure, and highly available managed database solutions on AWS. This milestone reinforces Comprinno’s commitment to enabling businesses to modernize their database workloads with operational efficiency and performance optimization.

The Amazon RDS Service Delivery badge validates Comprinno’s proven capabilities in designing, implementing, and managing Amazon RDS solutions, with specialization in Amazon RDS MySQL and Amazon RDS PostgreSQL engines. Comprinno empowers customers to offload time-consuming database management tasks such as backups, patching, monitoring, and scaling, while ensuring high availability, security, and cost optimization.

As an AWS Service Delivery Partner for Amazon RDS, Comprinno helps organizations accelerate cloud adoption by building resilient and performance-optimized database architectures. With deep expertise in database migration, performance tuning, and automation, Comprinno enables customers to achieve operational excellence and drive faster innovation on AWS.
													![Image](/images/blog/Prasad-PNG-Right-facing-e1765347228830-1024x909.png)

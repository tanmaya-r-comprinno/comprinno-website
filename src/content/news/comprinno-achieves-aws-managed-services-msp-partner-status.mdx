## Announcement
					
## Comprinno earns AWS Managed Services (MSP) Partner status
							
							
													
										28th Oct , 2024
									
								
													
										Bengaluru, IN
									
						
																	![Image](/images/blog/AWS_MSP.png)													We are excited to announce that Comprinno has officially achieved the prestigious AWS Managed Service Provider (MSP) Partner status. This significant milestone is a testament to our unwavering dedication to delivering the highest standards of managed cloud services to our clients. This achievement comes after months of consistent efforts and further solidifies our position as a trusted partner for businesses seeking to optimize their cloud infrastructure. The AWS MSP has been awarded to only 10 other companies globally in the last decade and this Partner status highlights our expertise in providing end-to-end, innovative cloud solutions that empower clients to maximize their cloud investments while ensuring security, scalability, and operational excellence.													![Image](/images/blog/1.png)													
“Achieving AWS MSP Partner status is an important milestone for us. It reinforces our commitment to delivering world-class managed cloud services, helping clients navigate the complexities of cloud adoption and management. This recognition strengthens our ability to drive operational efficiencies and business transformation for our customers.”

The AWS MSP Partner status is awarded to companies that demonstrate deep expertise in providing proactive cloud management and monitoring services, as well as the ability to optimize AWS environments for operational performance, cost efficiency, and security. This recognition highlights Comprinno’s growth and expertise in managing complex cloud infrastructures for businesses across various industries.

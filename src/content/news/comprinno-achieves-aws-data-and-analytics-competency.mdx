## Announcement
					
## Comprinno Achieves AWS Data and Analytics Competency
							
							
													
										June 17th July, 2026
									
								
													
										Bengaluru, IN
									
						
																	![Image](/images/blog/AWS-partner-DA-932x1024.webp)													
We are excited to announce that Comprinno has officially achieved the **AWS Data and Analytics Competency**. This significant milestone recognizes our expertise in delivering end-to-end modern data solutions on AWS, enabling organizations to build modern data platforms that drive business insights and innovation. This achievement reinforces our position as a trusted AWS Partner, helping customers design, implement, and manage scalable, secure, and production-ready analytics workloads.

The competency is awarded to AWS Partners that demonstrate deep technical proficiency and a proven track record of customer success in solving complex data and analytics challenges. By attaining this competency, Comprinno validates its capabilities in data ingestion, data engineering, data governance, data lakes, data warehousing, real-time analytics, business intelligence, visualization, cloud data migrations, and scalable analytics solutions built on AWS.
													![Image](/images/blog/1.png)													
"Achieving the AWS Data and Analytics Competency is a significant milestone for Comprinno. It reflects our commitment to helping organizations transform data into meaningful business insights through scalable, secure, and cloud-native analytics solutions. This recognition validates our expertise in designing modern data platforms on AWS that empower businesses to make faster, data-driven decisions while supporting innovation and long-term growth."

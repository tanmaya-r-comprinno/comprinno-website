## Announcement
					
## Comprinno earns AWS Cloud Operations Software Competency for Tevico Platform
							
							
													
										23rd Dec, 2024
									
								
													
										Bengaluru, IN
									
						
																	![Image](/images/blog/AWS_Cloud_Operations_Competency.png)													
Comprinno proudly announces that Tevico, our cloud governance SaaS platform, has achieved the AWS Cloud Operations Software Competency, specifically in the categories of Cloud Financial Management and Compliance & Auditing. This recognition highlights Tevico’s excellence in enabling businesses to optimize cloud costs, ensure regulatory compliance, and maintain secure, scalable AWS environments. The AWS Cloud Operations Software Competency is awarded to solutions demonstrating technical proficiency and proven success in helping organizations manage their cloud operations effectively. This milestone highlights Tevico’s capabilities as an innovative solution designed to address critical business needs, including financial governance and compliance management.
													![Image](/images/blog/1.png)													
“Achieving the AWS Cloud Operations Software Competency for Tevico is a proud moment for us. It reflects our dedication to creating solutions that empower businesses to achieve cost efficiency and regulatory excellence in their cloud operations. Powered by AWS’s advanced capabilities, Tevico helps clients unlock the full potential of their cloud investments.”

## Announcement
					
## Tevico 2.0 transforms AWS Well-Architected Reviews with a Gamified Review Module
							
							
													
										6th Dec, 2022
									
								
													
										Bengaluru, IN
									
						
																	![Image](/images/blog/Tevico-2-0-932x1024.webp)													
**Comprinno today announced the launch of Tevico 2.0** – which features a revolutionary enhancement to its Well-Architected Framework Review (WAFR) module. Version 2.0 reimagines the traditional review process, offering a more engaging, intelligent, & immersive experience for AWS customers & partners.
			
## Here’s what’s new with Tevico 2.0:
		
**AI-Powered Auto-Discovery :** ML & deep AWS integration for accurate risk assessments across all Well-Architected pillars.
**Gamified Reviews:** A points system with achievements, leaderboards, & challenges to boost engagement & speed up learning.
**Smart Remediation Plans:** Live editing, comments, & virtual whiteboarding for seamless team coordination.
**Real-Time Collaboration Hub:** A points system with achievements, leaderboards, & challenges to boost engagement & speed up learning.
**Visual Architecture Mapping:** Real-time collaborative diagram editing with popular tools.
**Contextual Learning Modules:** Interactive, role-based lessons on Well-Architected best practices tailored to your gaps.
**Dynamic Dashboard:** Customizable, real-time reports on architecture health, progress, & improvement opportunities.
			
## Looking to test-drive Tevico 2.0 ?
		
Tevico 2.0 is available immediately – for all existing customers. New users are eligible for a 30-day free trial

Learn more about this at [**www.tevi.co**](http://www.tevi.co).

“Tevico 2.0 marks a pivotal moment in the evolution of Well-Architected Reviews. We’ve addressed the longstanding challenges of technical complexity, limited adoption, & the need for intuitive capabilities like auto-discovery.

 

To elaborate – with Version 2.0, we can capture persona-specific observations like that of a Solution Architect, & showcase recommendations accordingly. The result is a more user-friendly & granular WAFR Module that simplifies the review process, while making it more rewarding & immersive.”

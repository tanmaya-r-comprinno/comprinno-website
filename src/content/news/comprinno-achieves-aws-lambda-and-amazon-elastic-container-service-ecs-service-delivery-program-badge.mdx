## Announcement
					
## Comprinno earns AWS Lambda and Amazon Elastic Container Service (ECS) Service Delivery Program badge
							
							
													
										7th March, 2024
									
						
																	![Image](/images/blog/Group-66264.png)													
Comprinno Technologies is delighted to announce that we have achieved AWS Lambda and Amazon ECS Service Delivery Program badges. This accomplishment signifies our dedication to delivering state-of-the-art modernization solutions that leverage the power of **serverless computing with AWS Lambda and container orchestration with Amazon ECS (ECS).**

As a distinguished AWS Partner with these service delivery programs, Comprinno stands at the forefront of assisting customers in their infrastructure and application modernization journey.

The AWS Lambda SDP combined with our Amazon API Gateway SDP, recognizes our expertise in building, deploying, and managing serverless applications, enabling developers to focus on core functionalities without the hassle of provisioning or managing servers.

Similarly, the Amazon ECS SDP badge highlights our proficiency in container orchestration with Amazon ECS. Our Well-Architected ECS and EKS solutions are a testament to our commitment to helping organizations modernize their infrastructure.

Partnering with Comprinno brings forth a multitude of benefits for businesses:
													![Image](/images/blog/1.png)													
“We are committed to helping customers in their migration and modernization journeys and the achievement of AWS Lambda and Amazon ECS SDPs is a testament to that,” said **Prasad Puranik, CEO** at Comprinno Technologies. “This recognition validates our ability to help businesses implement scalable, cost-effective solutions, and meet the modernization needs of our clients.”

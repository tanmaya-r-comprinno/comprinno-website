## Announcement
					
## Comprinno acquires AWS Gen AI Competency Partner status
							
							
													
										03rd Dec, 2024
									
								
													
										Bengaluru, IN
									
						
																	![Image](/images/blog/AWS_Generative_AI_competency.png)													
We are thrilled to announce that Comprinno has officially achieved the prestigious Amazon Web Services (AWS) Gen AI Competency Partner status! This accomplishment comes after months of focused efforts, further solidifying our position as a trusted leader in the AI and cloud services space. This milestone follows our recent success in attaining the AWS Managed Service Provider (MSP) Partner status, and together, these accolades reflect the significant strides we’ve made in advancing our Applied AI practice. Our team has been relentlessly working towards building high-impact, generative AI solutions that help drive innovation and deliver exceptional results for our customers.
													![Image](/images/blog/Prasad-PNG-Right-facing-e1765347228830-1024x909.png)													
“Becoming an AWS Gen AI Competency Partner is a momentous achievement for us. It underscores our commitment to leveraging the latest in generative AI technology to help our clients stay ahead of the curve. Our deep expertise, combined with AWS’s cutting-edge capabilities, allows us to deliver groundbreaking solutions that address the most complex challenges our customers face.” The AWS Gen AI Competency designation is awarded to partners who demonstrate technical proficiency and proven success in helping customers build solutions using AWS’s generative AI services. This recognition highlights Comprinno’s continued growth and unwavering dedication to delivering innovative, AI-driven solutions that create real-world impact.

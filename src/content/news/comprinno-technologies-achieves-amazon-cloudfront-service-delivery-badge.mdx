## Announcement
					
## Comprinno Technologies gains Amazon CloudFront Service Delivery Badge
							
							
													
										04th March, 2025
									
								
													
										Bengaluru, IN
									
						
																	![Image](/images/blog/Amazon_CloudFront_delivery.png)													
Comprinno Technologies is pleased to announce that it has attained the Amazon CloudFront Service Delivery badge, a recognition of its expertise in delivering secure, high-performance, and scalable content delivery solutions. This milestone reinforces Comprinno’s position as a trusted AWS Advanced Services Partner specializing in optimizing AWS architectures for speed, security, and efficiency.

The Amazon CloudFront Service Delivery badge highlights Comprinno’s proficiency in implementing low-latency content delivery networks (CDN) that ensure seamless performance for web applications, APIs, and media streaming. By leveraging AWS’s global edge locations, Comprinno enables businesses to optimize caching strategies, reduce latency, and improve security with AWS Shield and AWS WAF integration.

As an AWS Service Delivery Partner, Comprinno is committed to helping businesses enhance content delivery with cost-efficient, scalable, and secure solutions. Its expertise in fine-tuning CloudFront configurations ensures customers benefit from accelerated website performance and improved end-user experience.
